theLOOP COMPANY PROFILE

The Idea.

Founded in 2000 by Alan & David HsiatheLOOP is Asia’s only entertainment and experience design company that connects people to experiences through its award-winning concepts. Its customer first philosophy promotes its establishments as more than just real estate and instead aims to alter the cognitive perceptions of its guests through elevated customer service delivered by an elite in-house team built of the best people in their fields. The globally-recognized lifestyle enterprise has two decades of experience developing, owning and operating nightclubs, lounges and music festivals often in alignment with many of the world’s most high-profile entertainment brands. Together they have set a new institutional standard in lifestyle which transcends expectations and turns them into transformative experiences invigorated with the essence of Asia.

01 / theLOOP SINCE 2000

A vision to propel Taiwan’s nightlife industry.

When Alan & David Hsia launched theLOOP in 2000, they had a vision to propel Taiwan’s nightlife industry into something that would forever change the landscape in Asia. 16 years later theLOOP has matured into world-class experience design and lifestyle brand with a rich portfolio of entertainment, lifestyle and hospitality concepts. theLOOP have booked over 650 A-list acts from around the world, founded six large-scale nightclubs in three cities across two countries, thrown 36 festival-size events with an attendance spanning 10,000 – 32,000 partygoers. They have also collaborated with some of the world’s most celebrated entertainment brands, mixologists and chefs and have collected numerous awards and accolades including the prestigious Red Dot: Best of the Best award. The goal is to now build upon that success and will debut a host of new concepts in the coming years, with eyes on culinary driven concept lounges, boutique hotels and spas aimed at redefining the experience of each.
